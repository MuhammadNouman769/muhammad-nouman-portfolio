Privacy

A short, plain-language description of what this site records.

What I record

To understand which pages are useful, the site records each page you open: the page address, the time, the site you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate country, and your IP address. A first-party cookie named nv_id (random, no personal data) lets me tell a returning visitor from a new one. It lasts one year.

What I do not do

I do not sell or share this data, run advertising, or load third-party trackers. The only outside request this site makes is to Google Fonts for typefaces.

The contact form

If you send a message, I store your name, email, subject and message so I can reply. I delete messages that are no longer needed.
