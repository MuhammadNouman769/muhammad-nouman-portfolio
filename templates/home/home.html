{# 5 ─ HOW I WORK: a real sequence, line draws as you scroll #}

How I work with a team

The same four steps whether it is a feature or a whole platform. Nothing exotic, just done carefully.

  1. 1

    Understand the business

    What does done mean, who uses it, what breaks the company if it fails. Constraints first, code second.

  2. 2

    Model the data

    Schema, permissions and tenancy decided early, because they are the expensive things to change later.

  3. 3

    Build in thin slices

    Documented API contract, tests next to the code, and something you can click every week.

  4. 4

    Ship and watch

    Docker, CI, HTTPS, backups and logs. A release is not finished until it is running and observable.
