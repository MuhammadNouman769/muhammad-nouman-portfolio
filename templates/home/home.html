{# 6 ─ PROCESS #}
How we work

Four steps, whether it is a feature or a platform.

  1. 01

    Understand the business

    What done means, who uses it, what breaks if it fails. Constraints first, code second.

  2. 02

    Model the data

    Schema, permissions and tenancy decided early, because they are the costly things to change.

  3. 03

    Build in thin slices

    A documented API, tests beside the code, and something you can click every week.

  4. 04

    Ship and watch

    Docker, CI, HTTPS, backups and logs. A release is finished when it is running and observable.
